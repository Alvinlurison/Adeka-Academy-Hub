🔒 Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect your full name, email address, phone number, password, and the role you select (student, tutor, teacher, etc.). If you add one, your profile picture is also stored. Anything you create in the app — notes, quiz scores, class chat and private messages (including voice notes and photos), Office documents/spreadsheets, and questions you ask the AI Tutor — is stored so it's there when you come back.

How your data is used

Your data is used to run the app: signing you in, showing your name and picture to others you chat with, saving your work, and tracking quiz results for the leaderboard. We do not sell your data, and we do not show ads or let advertisers access any of it.

Where it's stored

Accounts, messages, notes, and files are stored using Google Firebase, Google's app backend service, and transmitted over HTTPS. If you use the AI Tutor, the text (and any photo or file you attach) is sent to Google's Gemini AI service to generate a response — nothing else is shared with it. If you add your own personal AI key in Settings, that key stays only on your device.

Payments

If you pay for personal AI access, that payment is handled entirely by Paystack, a licensed payment processor — we never see or store your card or mobile money details ourselves.

Students & young users

This app is built for learners of all ages, including students in JHS and below. We only collect what's needed for the app to function, and we encourage parents, guardians, and schools to stay involved in how students use it.

Your choices

You can update your name, role, phone number, and profile picture any time in Settings. To request that your account and data be deleted entirely, contact us using the details below — we don't yet have an automatic self-delete button, so this is handled manually for now.

Changes to this policy

As the app grows, this policy may be updated to reflect new features. Significant changes will be reflected here with an updated date at the top.

Contact

Questions about your data or this policy? Reach out at Alvinlurison@gmail.com or the phone numbers listed on the Dashboard.
