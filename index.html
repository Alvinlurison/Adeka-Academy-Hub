❓ Help & Getting Started

Creating Your Account

1. Enter your Full Name, Email, and a Password.
2. Tap "Create Account".
3. You're in — your dashboard loads right away.

Logging In Again

1. Enter the same Email and Password you signed up with.
2. Tick "Remember Me" so your email is saved for next time.
3. Tap "Login".

Changing Your Profile Picture

Open the ☰ Menu → ⚙️ Settings → "Choose New Picture".
